## What belongs here

- A specific situation you personally encountered, and what you did about it
- A workflow or prompting pattern that consistently gets better results
- A non-obvious feature or shortcut that saves time

## What doesn't belong here

- Anything already covered by [docs.devin.ai](https://docs.devin.ai) — link to
  the official docs and add only what they don't cover
- Vague advice like "be specific in your prompts" — show the actual prompt and
  the actual result
- Hypotheticals — only share what you've actually done
