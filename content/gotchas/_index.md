## What belongs here

- A specific behavior that surprised you, with the context that triggered it
- A workaround if you found one (or a clear statement that there isn't one yet)
- Enough detail that someone else can recognize the same situation

## What doesn't belong here

- Bug reports — file those with Cognition support or on their official channels
- Rants without substance — "Devin is bad at X" is not a gotcha; "Devin drops
  imports when refactoring files over 500 lines, here's the pattern I see and
  what I do about it" is
- Things that are documented behavior (that's not a gotcha, that's the docs)
