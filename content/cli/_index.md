# Devin CLI

The Devin CLI is the terminal-based experience — Devin running headless in your
shell, perfect for automation, scripting, CI/CD integration, and quick tasks
without leaving the terminal.

This section covers tips, workflows, and gotchas specific to the CLI surface.

## What we're collecting

- **Tips** — CLI patterns, automation recipes, and integration strategies.
- **Gotchas** — CLI-specific quirks, auth flows, and edge cases.
