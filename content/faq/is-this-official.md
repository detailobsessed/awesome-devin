No. Awesome Devin is a community project, maintained by volunteers and not
affiliated with or endorsed by Cognition AI.

The official resources are:

- **[docs.devin.ai](https://docs.devin.ai)** — the canonical documentation
- **[cognition.ai](https://cognition.ai)** — the company and its blog
- **In-app support** — the Help (?) icon at the bottom of the sidebar in
  [app.devin.ai](https://app.devin.ai)

This site's job is to cover what the docs don't: community-observed behavior,
honest gotchas, real configs, and answers people actually need. Everything here
is CC0 (public domain) — take it, remix it, contribute back if you can.

## Common follow-ups

**Then why trust it?** Fair question. The bar we hold content to is on the
[Contributing page](../contributing/) — real situations, honest limitations,
and a strict "link, don't duplicate" rule for anything the official docs cover.
