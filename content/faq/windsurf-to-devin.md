**Short answer:** On June 2, 2026, Windsurf became Devin Desktop and Cascade
became Devin Local. Your settings, rules, extensions, plan, and pricing all
carried over. Nothing you have today needs to change.

**The canonical reference is the official
[Devin Desktop FAQ](https://docs.devin.ai/desktop/devin-desktop-faq)** — it
covers the rename, Devin Local, rules compatibility, the `surf` →
`devin-desktop` command, enterprise allowlists, and more. We won't restate it
here; if the official FAQ answers your question, that's the answer.
